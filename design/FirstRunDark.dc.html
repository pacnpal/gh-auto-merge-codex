What counts as a go-ahead
  1. 1 A thumbs-up from Codex on the pull request, or its review receipt saying no major issues for the current head.
  2. 2 Newer than the latest commit. A push or re-run check after the go-ahead voids it, using GitHub's own timestamps.
  3. 3 No conflicts, no hold. Conflicting pull requests get one comment. Anything labeled do-not-merge is left alone.
